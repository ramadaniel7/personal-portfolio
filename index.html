About Me

The Person Behind
the Pixels & Data

I am a professional who combines two core expertise areas: precise technical data management and a dynamic digital creative industry instinct. Since 2021, I have been actively innovating in digital content creation, focusing on video editing, visual storytelling, and graphic design tailored to Gen Z and Gen Alpha audience trends.

In the technical and operational realm, I have a track record as a Data Validation Coordinator accustomed to managing large-scale workflows, handling over 512,000+ government administration records, and ensuring strict Quality Control (QC). This experience is supported by my capabilities in Web Development, script automation, and security system design — including the development of VEX-SYSTEM, an anti-cheat system for Roblox games.

This combined background makes me a Creative Specialist who can execute edgy, bold, and engaging visual ideas — as seen in the Superego x Hushbeast collaboration or TwinsBasketball commercial production — while working with structure, execution speed, and efficient project management.
